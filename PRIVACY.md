# Privacy Policy — CodeMac++ Mobile

**Last updated: March 25, 2026**

Antonio Scognamiglio ("we", "us", "our") operates the CodeMac++ Mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle your information when you use the App.

## Summary

**CodeMac++ does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.** The App runs entirely on your device. We have no servers, no analytics, no tracking, and no user accounts.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- No personal information (name, email, phone)
- No usage analytics or telemetry
- No crash reports sent to us
- No device identifiers
- No location data
- No browsing history
- No advertising identifiers

## Files and Documents

All files you open, edit, and save in CodeMac++ remain on your device or in your iCloud Drive. We never access, read, copy, or transmit your files to any server.

## AI Assistant (Optional, BYOK)

CodeMac++ includes an **optional** AI Assistant feature that is disabled by default. If you choose to enable it:

### How It Works
- You provide your own API key ("Bring Your Own Key" — BYOK) for one of the supported AI providers:
  - **Anthropic** (Claude)
  - **OpenAI** (GPT)
  - **Google** (Gemini)
- When you explicitly request an AI action (e.g., "Explain Code", "Refactor"), the **selected code or text** is sent directly from your device to the AI provider's API.

### What We Send
- Only the code/text you explicitly select for an AI action
- Your API key (sent directly to the provider, not through our servers)
- The AI action type (e.g., "explain", "refactor")

### What We Do NOT Send
- We do not send any data to our own servers — **we have no servers**
- We do not intercept, log, copy, or store API requests or responses
- We do not send your API key anywhere except to the provider you selected
- We do not send any code unless you explicitly tap an AI action

### API Key Storage
- Your API key is stored **exclusively in the iOS Keychain** on your device
- It is never stored in UserDefaults, files, or iCloud
- It is never transmitted to us or any third party other than the AI provider you chose
- You can delete your API key at any time from Settings

### Third-Party AI Providers
When you use the AI Assistant, your data is subject to the privacy policies of the AI provider you selected:
- Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy
- OpenAI: https://openai.com/privacy
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**We strongly recommend reviewing these policies before using the AI feature.**

### Consent
- A consent dialog is shown before the first use of the AI feature
- You must explicitly accept before any data is sent
- You can disable the AI feature at any time in Settings

## iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled on your device, CodeMac++ may sync the following **non-personal** preferences via iCloud:
- Theme selection
- Font size and family
- Editor settings (tab width, word wrap, etc.)
- Recent file bookmarks

**No API keys, file contents, or personal data are synced via iCloud.**

## Widgets

The CodeMac++ Recent Files widget displays filenames of recently opened files. This data is stored locally in an App Group container shared between the app and the widget. No data is transmitted externally.

## Spotlight Search

CodeMac++ indexes filenames and short content snippets of opened files in the iOS Spotlight index. This data remains on your device and is managed by iOS. We do not access or transmit Spotlight data.

## Children's Privacy

CodeMac++ does not knowingly collect any information from children. The App does not require any personal information to function.
